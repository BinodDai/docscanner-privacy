Mero Hisab

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 3, 2026

Mero Hisab is a personal finance management application designed to help users track expenses, income, transfers, and account balances.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is accessed, processed, stored, and managed when using Mero Hisab.

1. Information We Process

Mero Hisab may process financial information including:

  • Transaction amounts and dates
  • Income, expenses, and transfers
  • Account names and balances
  • Transaction categories and notes
  • Financial notification content
  • Receipt images selected by the user

2. Notification Access

With your permission, Mero Hisab uses Android Notification Access to detect transaction alerts from supported banking and digital wallet applications.

Notification content may be processed to identify transaction amounts, dates, transaction types, and other relevant details. Identified transaction details may be stored in the application's local database.

Notification access is optional. Users can revoke this permission at any time through Android system settings.

Because Android notification access can expose notification content from multiple applications, users should enable this permission only when they understand its purpose.

3. Local Data Storage

Mero Hisab is designed to store financial records locally on your device using an application database.

Financial information is used to display transaction history, account balances, spending summaries, and other personal finance features.

4. Receipt Scanning and OCR

When you choose to scan or attach a receipt, Mero Hisab may use optical character recognition (OCR) to extract relevant transaction information.

Receipt processing is intended to occur on your device. Users can review transaction information before saving.

5. How Information Is Used

  • To record and organize financial transactions
  • To calculate and display account balances
  • To categorize spending and income
  • To provide transaction search and filtering
  • To identify transactions from supported notifications
  • To support receipt scanning and manual entry
  • To generate user-requested exports

6. Data Sharing

Mero Hisab is designed to keep financial records on the user's device and does not require a developer-operated server for financial transaction storage.

Information may leave the device when users intentionally export, back up, or share their financial records through another application or service.

We do not sell personal financial information.

7. Permissions

Depending on the features used, Mero Hisab may request:

  • Notification Access: To recognize supported financial transaction notifications.
  • Camera: To capture receipt images when requested.
  • Photo or File Access: To select receipt images or import relevant documents.

Permissions may be denied or revoked through Android settings. Some features may not function without their required permissions.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Financial records remain stored on your device until you delete them, clear the application's storage, or uninstall the application.

Exported files and external backups must be deleted separately from their respective storage locations.

9. Data Security

Mero Hisab is designed with a focus on user privacy and local financial data management.

Users are encouraged to secure their devices using screen locks, biometric authentication where available, and updated Android security software.

No method of electronic storage can guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's Privacy

Mero Hisab is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to application features, functionality, or applicable privacy requirements.

Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the support email listed on the Mero Hisab Google Play Store page.
