# Privacy Policy for DocScanner

**Effective Date:** July 28, 2026
**Last Updated:** July 28, 2026

DocScanner is developed by **Binod Basnet** (“we,” “our,” or “us”). This Privacy Policy explains how information is accessed, processed, stored and shared when you use the DocScanner mobile application.

## 1. Overview

DocScanner is an offline-first document-scanning application. Document scanning, image processing, PDF creation, text recognition and document searching are performed locally on the user’s device.

We do not operate servers that receive or store users’ scanned documents, document images, generated PDF files or recognized OCR text.

DocScanner displays advertisements using Google AdMob. AdMob requires an internet connection and may collect limited device and usage information as described in this policy.

## 2. Documents and OCR data

DocScanner may locally process and store:

* Photographs captured using the camera
* Images selected by the user
* Scanned document pages
* Generated PDF files
* Text recognized through optical character recognition
* Document names, creation dates and organization information
* Local application preferences and security settings

This information is used only to provide DocScanner’s document-management functionality.

Scanned documents, images, PDF files and recognized text are not uploaded to our servers or provided to Google AdMob.

## 3. Local processing and storage

### Document scanning and OCR

Image processing and text recognition are performed on the device using local software libraries, including Google ML Kit where applicable.

If the application uses a downloadable ML Kit recognition model, an internet connection may temporarily be required to download that model. Document content is still processed locally and is not submitted to us.

### Local storage

Documents and related information are stored in DocScanner’s private application storage. Other applications cannot directly access this storage through normal Android application permissions.

A document may leave private application storage when the user intentionally exports, opens or shares it with another application.

### Cloud services

DocScanner does not provide developer-operated cloud storage or document synchronization.

Device-level backup services controlled by Android, Google or the device manufacturer may operate according to the user’s device settings. We do not control those services.

## 4. Advertising and third-party data collection

DocScanner uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK, commonly known as Google AdMob, to display advertisements and support application development.

According to Google, the Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share information such as:

* IP address, which may be used to estimate general location
* App interactions, including app launches, taps and advertisement interactions
* Diagnostic information, including performance and crash-related information
* Device and account identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID and App Set ID where available
* Device information required for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention

This information may be used by Google for advertising, analytics, service delivery and fraud prevention.

AdMob does not receive the contents of scanned documents, imported images, generated PDF files or recognized OCR text from DocScanner.

Google handles information according to its own Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

Users can manage advertisement personalization through their Android or Google advertising settings.

Where legally required, DocScanner may present consent options for personalized or non-personalized advertising before requesting advertisements.

## 5. Permissions

DocScanner may request the following Android permissions or system access:

### Camera

Used when the user chooses to photograph and scan a physical document.

### Photos and media

Used when the user chooses to import an image or save or export a document. On supported Android versions, DocScanner may use Android’s system photo or file picker instead of requesting broad storage access.

### Biometric authentication

Used to provide optional fingerprint or face authentication for the application lock.

Biometric authentication is performed by the Android operating system. DocScanner does not receive, collect or store fingerprint images, facial images or biometric templates.

### Internet access

Used to load Google AdMob advertisements, obtain required advertising consent information and, where applicable, download required on-device OCR models.

Internet access is not used to upload scanned documents, PDF files, imported images or recognized text to our servers.

### Notifications

If enabled, notifications may be used for application-related reminders or document-processing status. Users may disable notifications through Android Settings.

Permissions can be reviewed or revoked through:

**Android Settings → Apps → DocScanner → Permissions**

Some application features may not work when the corresponding permission is denied.

## 6. Security

DocScanner uses security measures intended to protect locally stored documents, including:

* AES-256 encryption for protected documents and images
* Encryption keys protected using the Android Keystore system
* Optional PIN-based application locking
* Optional biometric authentication
* Screenshot restrictions on sensitive screens
* Android application sandboxing
* Document-deletion procedures

While these protections reduce the risk of unauthorized access, no storage or security system can guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Document deletion and retention

Documents, images, generated PDF files, recognized text and document metadata remain on the device until the user:

* Deletes the relevant document
* Clears DocScanner’s application storage
* Uninstalls DocScanner
* Uses another available deletion function

When a document is deleted, DocScanner removes its associated local files and database records. The application may attempt to overwrite applicable files before deletion.

Because Android devices commonly use flash storage, storage optimization, wear levelling and device backup systems, DocScanner cannot guarantee that deleted information will be unrecoverable under every forensic or backup scenario.

We do not retain copies of deleted documents on developer-operated servers because documents are not uploaded to such servers.

Information collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google’s own policies and retention practices.

## 8. User-initiated sharing

DocScanner allows users to open, export or share selected documents.

When the user selects one of these actions, Android provides the selected file to the application or service chosen by the user. Such sharing is initiated and controlled by the user.

Information shared with another application is governed by that application’s privacy policy. We are not responsible for the data-handling practices of applications selected by the user.

## 9. Sale of personal information

We do not sell users’ scanned documents, imported images, PDF files or recognized text.

Google’s handling of advertising-related information is governed by Google’s policies and the advertising choices provided to users.

## 10. Children’s privacy

DocScanner is not specifically designed or directed toward children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect children’s scanned documents or personal information on developer-operated servers. Advertising shown to users is subject to the application’s selected Google Play audience settings and Google’s advertising policies.

If DocScanner is made available to children, we will configure advertising and data practices according to the Google Play Families policies and applicable law.

## 11. Accounts

DocScanner does not require users to create an online account.

Because no online account is created and no document data is stored on our servers, there is no server-side account or document data for users to request that we delete.

Users can delete locally stored information through DocScanner, Android application settings or by uninstalling the application.

## 12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in DocScanner’s features, third-party services, legal requirements or data-handling practices.

Updates will be published on this page with a revised “Last Updated” date. Users are encouraged to review this policy periodically.
